## Overview

PlumbBros is a The Sims 4 mod that introduces a fully playable plumbing and home repair business career, letting Sims accept client service calls, travel to residential lots to complete repairs, earn revenue based on job quality and reputation, and build a growing handyman company from a solo operation to a multi-employee business.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **The Sims 4 PlumbBros** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Repair Business** | Launch and manage a Sim-owned plumbing and repair service company |
| **Client Job System** | Accept service calls and travel to client lots to complete repair work |
| **Revenue & Reputation** | Earnings scale with job quality and accumulated reputation rating |
| **Skill Integration** | Handiness and Maintenance skills directly affect earnings and success rate |
